# Paisa Safar · पैसा सफ़र

**Play free in your browser: https://selfishyellow.github.io/paisa-safar/**

Paisa Safar is a pixel-art Indian journey game where new investors practise spotting investment scams (WhatsApp tip groups, fake trading apps, fake KYC calls, deepfakes) before they cost real money. Every scam stop trains one habit: **रुको · जाँचो · फिर करो** (Pause · Verify · Then act), and links to the real tools: SEBI's registered-intermediary list, @valid UPI handles, SEBI SCORES and the 1930 cyber-fraud helpline.

- Free, no sign-up, about 5 minutes, Hindi + English
- All people, brands, apps and coins are fictional; no real money and no personal data

Built by Aryan Gour, Neham Raj, Harsh Tomar and Yuvraj (IIM Bodh Gaya) for CDSL Ideathon 2.0. Mentor: Dr Pinaz Tiwari.
